# Scoring rules (used by Level 2 and Level 3)

## Step 1: hard gates (any failure removes the candidate)
Each is scored 1–5. A score of **3 or less on any gate eliminates the candidate.**
- **F1 Ground reality:** works with cash, paper and basic phones.
- **F2 Saves money:** no new cost for the person who benefits.
- **F3 No gatekeepers or lanjam:** the person who benefits is the only decision-maker, with no official, office or committee involved.

## Step 2: weighted score (survivors only)
| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| F1 Ground reality | 15% | A paper slip or voice call is enough. Nothing breaks for cash users |
| F2 Saves money | 20% | Free for the person who benefits, with a large and visible saving (₹ thousands) |
| F3 No gatekeepers | 20% | Nobody's approval is ever needed, and no powerful party can block it |
| F4 Not crowded | 15% | Searches find no neutral player in India. Only adjacent or biased tools exist |
| F5 Practical for a solo developer | 20% | MVP in 2 weeks for under ₹50k, a 2-week validation test, and a clear first revenue |
| F6 Cheap to market | 10% | Spreads naturally via WhatsApp and Tamil reels, and is tied to a moment people already care about |

**Weighted score** = Σ(score × weight), with a maximum of 5.00.

## Step 3: tie-breakers
1. Strength of evidence that the pain is real (sources, not assumptions).
2. How soon the first rupee of revenue can arrive.
3. Timing (for example, festival or wedding seasons in the next 3 months).
