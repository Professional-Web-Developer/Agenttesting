## The founder
- A software developer (MCA graduate) based in **Tamil Nadu, India**.
- Wants **one practical business idea** that solves a real problem for people, ideally something not solved before, together with a marketing plan.
- Has little capital (**under ₹50,000 to start**) and can build software alone (web, WhatsApp bots, simple backends).
- Is very practical and sceptical, and has already **rejected four ideas**. The filters below come directly from those rejections.

## Filters every idea MUST pass
| # | Filter | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Works with ground reality.** Must work when people pay in **cash**, keep **paper** records, use basic phones or have low digital literacy. At most, one family member uses WhatsApp. Must not depend on UPI, smart meters, apps being installed, etc. | Rejected "Saakshi" (work history for domestic workers built from UPI salary payments) because many workers are paid in cash |
| F2 | **Saves the customer money. No new cost.** People are cutting costs. Best: free for the person who benefits, with money coming from another party, or paid **only from savings**. | Rejected "NeevCheck" (paid engineer inspections during house construction) because it added a cost |
| F3 | **No gatekeepers and no lanjam (bribery).** Must NOT need approval, cooperation, licences or goodwill from officials, government offices, committees (RWAs), schools, hospitals, or anyone who profits from corruption or kickbacks. **The person who benefits must be the only decision-maker.** | Rejected "SocietySaver" (cost cutting for apartment associations) because committee members take vendor kickbacks and would block it |
| F4 | **Not already crowded.** No strong existing neutral player in India. Verify with web searches and name every competitor you find. | Several earlier ideas already existed |
| F5 | **Practical for this founder.** A solo developer can build an MVP in 2–4 weeks for under ₹50k, validate it in about 2 weeks, and see a clear revenue model. | "Don't give me a nonsense idea" |
| F6 | **Cheap to market.** Customers can be reached via WhatsApp, Instagram/YouTube reels in Tamil, or local word of mouth. | The founder also wants the marketing done |

## Current front-runner (the idea to beat)
**Sedharam Check.** A family sends a WhatsApp photo of a jewellery bill or estimate slip. The tool converts it into one comparable number: the real premium over the day's standard 22K gold rate (22K is about ₹14,000/g today). It then shows verified cheaper shops for that item type. Free for families. Cheaper jewellers pay 0.5% per referred purchase. Full plan: `/home/user/Agenttesting/sedharam-check-plan.md`.
